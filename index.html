Studio

How we make apps

We're a small, senior team building products we want to use ourselves. A few rules we don't bend:

Made for everyday moments

Waking up, writing a message, checking your stars. We build for the small moments that happen every day.

Private by design

We collect only what an app needs to work, and we say plainly what that is.

Sweat the details

Motion, sound, and words tuned until they feel right, on every screen and in every language.

Here for the long run

We run what we ship. Updates, support, and fixes keep coming long after launch day.
